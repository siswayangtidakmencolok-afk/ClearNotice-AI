DIRECTORATE GENERAL OF HIGHER EDUCATION — REPUBLIC OF INDONESIA

Ministry of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology • Circular No. 0418/E2/DT.01/2026

National Student Innovation Grant (NSIG) Fiscal Year 2026 Call for Proposals & Statutory Operational Terms

ISSUANCE: JAKARTA • REF: E2-DT01-2026 • DISTRIBUTION: PUBLIC / HIGHER ED COUNCILS
[P01]

The Ministry of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology (MoECRT), through the Directorate General of Higher Education, hereby announces the official commencement of the National Student Innovation Grant (NSIG) Fiscal Year 2026. This flagship competitive initiative is instituted to accelerate institutional scientific research, cross-disciplinary technological entrepreneurship, and commercial prototypes developed by collegiate scholars across Indonesia.

[P02]

Eligible student proponents must be active undergraduate students (S1 / D4 degree tracks) enrolled in accredited public or private universities across Indonesia at the time of proposal intake. Candidate teams are required to submit interdisciplinary project teams consisting of a minimum of three (3) active undergraduates representing at least two distinct departments within STEAM disciplines.

[P03]
center_focus_strong Primary Evidence Focus Application window & financial provisions

Application period: April 15 – May 31, 2026.

Target of Evidence Ref [02] • External Caveat PORTAL DISCLOSURE DELEGATION

“Detailed technical guidelines, budget breakdown sheets, and institutional endorsement portals can be accessed through the official portal.”

[P04]

Formal proposal intake opens electronically on April 15, 2026. The official submission window closes irreversibly on May 31, 2026 at 23:59 Western Indonesia Time (WIB). Late submissions, incomplete documentation packets, or submissions failing to attach Dean endorsement letters will be automatically disqualified without right of administrative appeal.

[P05]

Institutions may nominate an unlimited number of preliminary student proposals; however, each individual student may only appear as Principal Project Lead on one (1) single grant submission. Duplicate lead filings will result in rejection of all associated dossiers.
